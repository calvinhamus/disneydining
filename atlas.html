    How to read the scores. Food is cooking quality relative to what else exists on property, not to restaurants in the real world. Immersion is how much the setting, theming, and service add to the meal. A 10 for immersion means the room is the reason to go. These are my synthesis of critical consensus and reporting, not measured data, so treat them as a strong starting opinion rather than fact.

    Booking. Reservations open 60 days out at 6:00am Eastern, and the hardest tables on property go in minutes: Victoria & Albert's, Cinderella's Royal Table, Space 220, and Topolino's breakfast. The top three fine dining rooms now charge a $100 per person no-show fee, and Monsieur Paul wants three days notice to cancel. Almost everything else can be picked up on a walk-up list or a same-day cancellation if you keep checking.

    Currency. Statuses reflect July 2026. Disney's construction pace means this shifts constantly, so confirm in My Disney Experience before you build a day around any one meal.
