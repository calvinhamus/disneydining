Which way round

Are you splitting pours

    Where to start. Mexico first is the classic lap if you come in the main entrance, since you hit the two strongest bars early. Canada first is better from the International Gateway or if you are staying at a BoardWalk-area resort, and it has the advantage of putting Germany's big pours near the end rather than at stop four.

    Standard drink estimates assume a US standard drink of 0.6 fl oz of pure alcohol. Pour sizes at World Showcase vary a lot and the counts here are approximations, not measurements. Germany's large stein alone is roughly three of them. Prices are estimates and move constantly.

    Practical stuff. The lap is about 1.2 miles, so it takes longer than people expect and that is a good thing. Eat somewhere in the middle, use the water stops, and take Disney transportation or a rideshare. Cast members will cut you off and can decline service, and nobody wants their day to end at first aid.
