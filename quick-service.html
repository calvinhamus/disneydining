    The cyan dot means I checked current menus and pricing for that counter. Entries without one are confirmed as taking a credit, but treat the order suggestions as a starting point.

    Before you order: menus and prices rotate constantly, and the 2026 roster shifted through the year, especially third-party Disney Springs locations. Confirm the dining-plan badge in My Disney Experience before committing. Magic Kingdom quick service pours no alcohol anywhere, which is why nothing there ranks near the top. Resort food courts hide rotating specialty entrees like pot roast, half chicken, and grilled salmon that never make the posted board, so ask. At several Disney-run counters you can upgrade plain fries to a specialty fry at no charge on the plan.

    Two traps: Pizza Pickup at Via Napoli costs two adult quick-service credits, not one. And Kusafiri Coffee Shop in Animal Kingdom serves a good plant-based Picadillo Bowl but is not on the current participating list, so bring cash for it.
