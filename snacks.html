    The one rule. A snack credit is worth about $6.50, so target anything above $7 and pay cash for everything below. Eligible items carry a small purple and white DDP symbol on the menu board. The same item can be a snack at one stand and not at another, so check the board rather than assuming.

    Alcohol is never a snack credit. Beer, wine, and cocktails can only be swapped onto a meal credit at 21+, never a snack. Souvenir cups, mugs, and popcorn buckets are also excluded, so the Night Blossom in a regular cup works and the glowing souvenir version does not.

    If a festival is running, stop reading and go there. EPCOT's four festivals cover most of the calendar, and nearly every food item and non-alcoholic drink at the booths takes a snack credit. Items normally get excluded above about $8, which is exactly why the $9 and $10 festival portions are the best conversion on property. Festival alcohol and most festival funnel cakes are excluded.

    Prices are estimates built from current reporting and they move constantly. Use them to rank options, not to budget to the dollar.
